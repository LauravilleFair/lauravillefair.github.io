Volunteer

Lend a hand on fair day

Our on-site volunteers work two-hour shifts around the park, from 8AM setup through 7PM breakdown. Pick what suits you:

  • Setup and breakdown
  • Trash collection
  • Vendor assistance and breaks
  • Entertainment and AV assistant
  • Photographer: capture the day, no special equipment needed

Kids can fill several roles (some ages need a parent along), and we're happy to sign off on service-learning hours.
