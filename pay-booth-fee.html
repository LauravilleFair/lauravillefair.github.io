Pay Your Booth Fee

Congratulations, and welcome to The Lauraville Fair!

Please only pay after you've received your acceptance email from The Lauraville Fair. Applying doesn't guarantee a spot.
  1. Find your category below and click its pay button. If you signed up for a Social Media Spotlight, add it at checkout.
  2. Enter your business or organization name at checkout so we can match your payment.
  3. Pay securely through Square by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Cash App Pay. You'll get a receipt by email.

Please pay by the deadline in your acceptance email. Can't pay online? Email Info@TheLauravilleFair.org and we'll work it out.
