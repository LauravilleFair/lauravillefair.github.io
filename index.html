The 41st Annual Lauraville Fair

Local makers, live music, and the whole neighborhood in one park

The Lauraville Fair is an annual festival that brings neighbors together with local artisan vendors, restaurants, and bands. Everything we do is about keeping it local, and The Lauraville Fair is 100% volunteer run.
