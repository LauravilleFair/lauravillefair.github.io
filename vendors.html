Vendor FAQ

When do I pay?

Only after you're selected. We'll send you an invoice. How invoices are paid and the payment deadline

What size are booth spaces?

Booths are 10 × 10 feet.

Do I need to bring my own tent and tables?

Yes. Vendors bring their own tent and tables.

When can I set up?

The fair runs 11AM–5PM. Setup begins at 8AM, and breakdown must be finished by 7PM.

Is electricity available?

No, electricity is not available.

What is the rain policy for vendors?

The Lauraville Fair happens rain or shine.
