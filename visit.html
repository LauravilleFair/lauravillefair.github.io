When & Where

Heinz Park, Baltimore

The last Saturday in September

Getting there

Parking is limited to the street, so leave the car at home if you can. We have bike racks at the park, bus stops are nearby, and Baltimore's rentable scooters and e-bikes are a great way to get here.

About

About the fair

For four decades, The Lauraville Fair has brought Lauraville and its neighbors together in Heinz Park. Today it is organized by a team of about a dozen volunteers, who took it over five years ago, and run by The Lauraville Fair Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our focus is simple: local artisans, local restaurants, local bands, and 100% volunteer power.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is The Lauraville Fair free?

Yes! The Lauraville Fair is free and open to everyone.

Where can I park?

Parking is limited to street parking around the park, so we strongly recommend walking, biking, or taking the bus. See Getting there above.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are welcome! Local animal shelters join us at the fair with pets available for adoption.

Is there food and drink?

Yes. Local restaurants and food vendors serve food all day, and we sell beer and wine.
